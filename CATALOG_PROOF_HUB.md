# Phoenix Catalog Proof developer hub

A single starting point for ecommerce migration QA, feed approval and client handover evidence.

## Choose your path

- [Run Phoenix Catalog Proof on Apify](https://apify.com/phoenix-tools/phoenix-catalog-proof) — compare two authorized catalog snapshots and download HTML, CSV and JSON evidence.
- [Copy-paste demo input](CATALOG_PROOF_DEMO.md) — see added, removed, price and stock findings.
- [API integration guide](CATALOG_PROOF_API_INTEGRATION.md) — automate runs from Bash, CI, Make, Zapier or webhooks with a cost ceiling.
- [Migration QA use case](https://github.com/PhoenixToolsDev/Phoenix-ImageFlow/issues/1) — practical catalog comparison example.
- [Agency handover use case](https://github.com/PhoenixToolsDev/Phoenix-ImageFlow/issues/2) — evidence checklist before client delivery.

## What it checks

Added or removed products, price/currency, availability, titles, duplicate identifiers, missing or broken image/product URLs, redirects and optional canonical mismatches.

Use only catalog data and URLs you own or are authorized to process. The Actor is read-only and does not require store credentials.
